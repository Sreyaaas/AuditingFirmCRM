# Enterprise Minimal UI & Practice Design Expert

## When to Use
- Building or refining UI components for Shameer & Associates CRM.
- Designing high-density, professional accounting, GST, ITR, and practice workflow interfaces.
- Eliminating generic AI slop (3D spheres, glassmorphic tilts, rainbow gradients) in favor of Apple/Vercel minimal enterprise precision.

## 🎯 Design Principles
- **Restraint & Purpose:** Every pixel serves operational clarity. Flat muted status tints, 1px hairline borders (`#E5E5EA` / `#2C2C2E`), and clean monospace tabular numbers.
- **Centered Modals:** Major dialogs (client details, tasks, tickets) must be centered windows (`max-w-3xl`), never right-side off-canvas drawers.
- **Authentic Practice Context:** Shameer & Associates (Accountants and Practitioners). Partners: Shameer & Ameen (NEVER use "CA"). Staff: Susmi.
- **OneDrive & SharePoint Integration:** Real auditing files (PDFs, Excel registers, JSON payloads).
